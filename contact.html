GRIEVANCE PATH STA-CONTACT · FAQ WRITTEN ONLY
01Who reads a complaint?

Write to briefs@stacklytalent.demo with the brief ID. A partner who did not run the search reads it within three working days. Demo inbox - no mail is delivered from this preview.

02What can be grieved?

A missed outcome to a candidate, a fee that was not on the retainer, or a scorecard that was ignored at debrief.

03What is not a grievance path?

A request to skip the brief, or to add unpaid trial work. Those are refused, not escalated.
