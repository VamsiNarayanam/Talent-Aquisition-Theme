Longread 12 Sep 2026

The scorecard is a record, not a vibe check.

A loop fails when each interviewer invents a private test. We publish the questions with the brief. The scale is 1 to 4. A 3 means the evidence met the bar. A 4 means it exceeded it on a named point. "Strong hire" with no sentence is sent back.

Disagreement stays in the document. If product and engineering mark the same exercise differently, the debrief names the gap. It does not average it into a polite maybe.

Field plates

Where the work sits.

Two places the desk actually uses - the room and the walk to it.

Plate 01

The room

Loops run in Indiranagar or on a timed call. The scorecard is open during the debrief, not after everyone has left.

  • Indiranagar desk or timed remote
  • Scorecard open in the room
  • Debrief before people leave

Plate 02

The commute

Candidates are told the station and the walk. We do not book a 9:00 slot and then move it twice.

  • Station + walk named in the invite
  • Slots hold once booked
  • No last-minute shuffle
