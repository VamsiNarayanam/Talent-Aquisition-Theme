Origin

Opened because shortlists had become attachments.

Meera Iyer started the desk in Indiranagar in 2019 after a year of hiring managers receiving forty CVs and no argument for any of them.

The rule was simple: no search without a written brief, and no shortlist without a score. Arjun joined for revenue roles. Leela built the ops and contract practice.

House rules

How a brief is allowed to move.

Four rules that stay on the desk. Break one and the brief pauses.

STA · RULEBOOK

HOUSE RULEBOOK STA-DEMO · 4 LOCKS IN FORCE
01 In force

Brief

The brief is the contract of attention.

If it is not written, it is not in scope. No search starts from a forwarded job post or a verbal ask alone.

02 In force

Signal

Candidates hear a yes, a no, or a date.

Silence is not a stage on this board. Every interviewed person gets a written outcome.

03 In force

Fees

Fees are written before work.

GST and the replacement window sit on the retainer. They are not negotiated after a shortlist lands.

04 In force

Scorecard

Notes live on the scorecard.

Side chats do not outrank the debrief. The same questions, the same bar, the same day.

Halt. Break one lock mid-search and the brief pauses until the owner puts it back in writing.

Will not

What this desk refuses.

Red lines, stamped at enquiry. These are not negotiable mid-search.

Declined

Ghosting candidates

Every person who interviewed receives a written outcome. A slow week is not an excuse.

Declined

Fake urgency

We will not invent a competing offer or a Friday deadline to force a decision.

Declined

Unpaid trial work

Take-home tasks that are really client work are declined. A short exercise, paid or clearly bounded, is the limit.

Declined

Search without an owner

If nobody can accept an offer, the brief stays in enquiry.
