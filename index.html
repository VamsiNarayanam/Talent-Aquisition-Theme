11 / FAQ

Before you file.

Straight answers on briefs, shortlists, retainers, and what this demo can and cannot do.

Do you work from a job description alone?

No. A job description is a start. Kickoff files the bar, the compensation band, the interviewer list, and who can say yes. Without that owner, the brief stays in enquiry.

How many names are on a shortlist?

Usually five to seven, each with a scorecard stub. We do not send a stack of CVs and call it a search. If the list feels thin, we narrow the bar before we add names.

Where do you hire?

India, and remote roles employed in India. Bengaluru and Hyderabad are the desks. Other cities are fine when the brief names the city, the entity, and the overlap hours.

What is an offer pack?

The role brief, the scorecard, the compensation band, notice and start, reference notes, and the decision. One document the hiring manager can stand behind before any verbal offer.

What does a ramped hire cover?

Up to three concurrent briefs inside one function, a weekly written note, and the same scorecard standard as a retained search. Product and revenue are separate retainers unless one owner and one band logic apply.

Do you replace a retained hire?

Yes, inside 90 days, if the person leaves or the brief was met and the seat is still the same. A rewritten city, bar, or title is a new brief - not a free rewrite of the old one.

Is the fee negotiable in this demo?

The public schedule is illustrative. A live retainer is confirmed in writing, with GST, before work starts. This site does not take payment and does not create a real mandate.
