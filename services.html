DESK LETTER STA-SVC · FAQ WRITTEN ONLY
01Can two lines share one retainer?

A ramped hire covers one function. Product and revenue are separate briefs unless the owner is the same and the band logic matches.

02Do you replace a retained hire?

Yes, inside 90 days, if the person leaves or the brief was met and the seat is still the same. A rewritten role is a new brief.

03Will you run a confidential search?

Yes, when the brief says who may be told. We still will not invent urgency to flush names.

04What starts the clock on a retained search?

The filed brief and the first retainer instalment. Outreach does not begin on a verbal ask or a forwarded job post alone.

05Do you work campus or contract seats?

As a project surge with an end date on the brief. We do not run an open mill of unpaid interns or an endless contractor funnel.
